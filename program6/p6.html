Ilahia College Of Engineering And Technology

Ilahia College of Engineering and Technology (ICET), established in 2002, is a pioneering self-financing engineering institution in Kerala, approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU). Over two decades of academic excellence, the institution has produced 21 batches of engineers who serve in key professional and entrepreneurial roles. ICET remains committed to inclusive education through scholarships and free education initiatives, fostering future leaders in engineering and technology.
